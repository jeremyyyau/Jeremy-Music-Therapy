# Elegant rhythmic card treatment

## What will change
- Introduce a reusable, very faint music-note and sound-wave pattern for the white information boxes across the site.
- Give icon boxes more presence with soft layered backgrounds, a small rhythmic dot detail, and restrained hover movement.
- Add a subtle bottom wave and short rhythmic accent to selected text-only boxes so they feel less plain without adding more words.
- Keep photographs, wording, typography, page structure, and the existing warm colour palette unchanged.

## Where it will be applied
- Home page therapy, services, care-value, and contact boxes.
- Music Therapy intervention, benefit, audience, and progress boxes.
- Services cards and practical-detail panels.
- About experience, testimonial, and instrument boxes.
- Resource tiles and the resource-sharing panel.

## Technical details
- Build the decoration once as a reusable presentational layer, then apply shared semantic classes to the existing boxes.
- Keep patterns behind content, low-opacity, non-interactive, and hidden from screen readers.
- Preserve readable contrast, touch behaviour, reduced-motion support, current wrapping safeguards, and card dimensions.
- Check phone, tablet, and desktop views, then confirm all pages still load without errors.
